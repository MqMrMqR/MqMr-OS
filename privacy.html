Inbox privacy

Updated October 1, 2026

Inbox is part of MqMr OS, operated by the owner of mqmr.bio. Contact: mqmrpc@gmail.com.

Site login and Google mail

Normal sign-in identifies your site account through Google and Supabase. It does not request Gmail permission. Connecting an account in Inbox is a separate choice. Inbox requests Gmail permission to display your messages, send replies and messages, and manage read status, stars, archive and recoverable Trash.

Where Gmail data goes

Google access tokens and fetched Gmail messages are held in memory in your browser. Inbox calls the Gmail API directly. This site's backend does not receive or store Gmail tokens or messages. Reloading or leaving the page clears the in-memory connection. Downloaded attachments are saved on your device when you choose Download.

Google processes your authorization and API requests. You may remove a connected account from this page or revoke this application's permissions from your Google account security settings. Removing an account from the page clears its token here but does not revoke Google's authorization.

Domain mail

Mail addressed to @mqmr.bio is handled by Cloudflare Email Routing, delivered to the MqMr OS Supabase backend and stored there, including sender, recipient, subject, bodies and attachments. Domain messages sent by its owner use Resend; Resend receives the message and attachments to deliver them. Domain mail is accessible only to the authorized owner account. Deleting to Trash is recoverable and does not permanently erase stored mail.

Preferences and access

Desktop layout and appearance preferences are stored on your device. Supabase maintains your signed-in session. Inbox does not use Gmail data for advertising, sell it, or share it with the site's backend. The underlying Google, Cloudflare, Supabase and Resend services have their own privacy policies.

For domain-mail access, export or deletion requests, contact the owner above. Gmail messages remain controlled by your Google account; you can manage or delete them directly in Gmail.
